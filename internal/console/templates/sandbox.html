Both providers are local fakes (SWIFTPAY_MODE=sandbox, MOMOBRIDGE_MODE=sandbox). Recipient details pick the outcome, see docs/sandbox.md. You can also force the next payout's outcome here.

Swiftpay (bank)

  • Account ending 0001: fails (INVALID_ACCOUNT)
  • Account ending 0099: compliance hold, then completes
  • Account ending 0077: completes, then the bank reverses it
  • Account ending 0055: completes without a webhook (the poller picks it up)
  • NGN amounts of ₦1,000,000 or more: queued for manual review, completes later

MoMoBridge (mobile money)

  • Number ending 0000: fails (SUBSCRIBER_NOT_FOUND)
  • Number ending 5555: settles without a callback
  • KES and UGX amounts must be whole shillings, like the live rails
