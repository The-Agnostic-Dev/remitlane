Swiftpay (bank)

  • Account ending 0001: fails (INVALID_ACCOUNT)
  • Account ending 0099: compliance hold, then completes
  • Account ending 0077: completes, then the bank reverses it
  • Account ending 0055: completes without a webhook (the poller picks it up)
  • NGN amounts of ₦1,000,000 or more: queued for manual review, completes later
